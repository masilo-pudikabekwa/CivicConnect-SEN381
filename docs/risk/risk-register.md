### Highest Priority Risk

**R-012**: Unsupported or immeasurable quality/NFR claims at the M1 defence.

This is the only critical entry because it impacts 3 separate rubric lines simultaneously (Requirements and Acceptance Criteria, PED Quality, and the individual engineering defence). It is also actively live; every Non-Functional Requirement (NFR) baselined without a measurable acceptance criterion carries this risk forward. It is fully within the team's control to close before sign-off, which is why the mitigation is to "reject the NFR and do not baseline it" rather than a softer control.
